# Mindful Focus

Build REBOOT as a premium mobile-first interactive web app that I will later convert to SwiftUI.

VISUAL SYSTEM:

Outside onboarding, use https://www.craft.do/ as the absolute design-quality reference: elegant editorial typography, extremely clean spacing, warm paper backgrounds, generous rounded widgets, pill buttons, soft tactile surfaces, subtle shadows, almost no borders, beautiful whitespace and immediate comprehensibility. Do NOT copy Craft branding/assets; transpose its visual grammar into REBOOT.

IMPORTANT: I am attaching 6 onboarding artworks named page1.png → page6.png.

USE THESE EXACT FILES. Do not regenerate, redraw, replace, crop aggressively or add fake AI brain imagery. They are the official REBOOT onboarding artwork.

The artworks are metaphorical branding visuals, NOT medical scans or measurements. Never claim the images show the user's actual brain, damage, dopamine levels, neural activity or biological recovery.

ONBOARDING MUST FEEL LIKE A PREMIUM CINEMATIC PRODUCT TRAILER, not a carousel of ordinary app cards.

Each artwork fills the screen edge-to-edge on a pure black background. Preserve the empty lower portions for typography and controls. Use elegant white/off-white typography with Signal Red only for tiny emphasis.

MOTION:

The six images should feel connected as one sequence.

Use 600–900ms cinematic crossfades with very subtle 1.02→1.00 scale, controlled image parallax and slow red-light breathing.

page1→page2: surrounding noise grows.

page2→page3: distractions disappear and attention visually narrows.

page3→page4: red intensity becomes cleaner/warmer.

page4→page5: reveal the progress arc.

page5→page6: arc collapses toward the bright central point, then page6 resolves.

Never use glitch, bouncing animations, spinning elements or cheap slideshow transitions.

Text enters after the artwork by ~120–180ms with opacity + 8–14px upward movement.

CTA remains low on screen and respects mobile safe areas.

TYPOGRAPHY:

For the cinematic onboarding use a beautiful modern sans-serif, large and extremely clean — bold display headings, restrained body.

Do NOT use hacker/monospace typography except tiny metadata such as “01 / 06”.

No giant blocks of ALL CAPS.

No outlined HUD components.

AFTER “BEGIN REBOOT”:

The black cinematic world should dissolve smoothly into REBOOT’s Craft-inspired product world:

warm off-white paper background,

soft large-radius cards,

editorial serif headlines,

clean sans-serif UI,

black pill controls,

Coral Red and subtle Cyan accents.

This transition should feel intentionally dramatic: from NOISE into CLARITY.

Immediately begin the adaptive diagnosis.

One question per screen, Craft-level simplicity and spacing.

First question:

“Why are you here?”

Choices:

Scroll less

Focus better

Deep work

Study better

Read more

Remember more

Build Flow

Use my phone less

Allow multiple choices, then ask:

“If REBOOT could change only one, which matters most?”

That becomes the explicit primary goal.

Branch the diagnosis from that answer instead of showing everyone the same questionnaire.

Ask only relevant questions about main distractor, social app, work/study problem, current focus window, reading/recall issues, environment and activities where the user naturally becomes absorbed.

Typically 8–14 total questions.

Unknown answers remain UNKNOWN — never fabricate defaults.

At the end show a beautiful Craft-style “Your starting point” report:

Primary goal

Main breaker

Current focus window

Known absorption context

Unknown dimensions

Status: Calibrating

Copy: “We start with what we know. The first week measures the rest.”

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE:

Do not build REBOOT as a dark cyberpunk dashboard because the onboarding is dark.

The onboarding is the cinematic wake-up moment.

The PRODUCT itself must feel calm, elegant, tactile, rounded and extraordinarily clear — much closer to Craft.do.

Build the full onboarding and diagnosis as a real clickable experience with persistent state, responsive mobile layouts and production-level motion.

Prioritize polish over speed.
